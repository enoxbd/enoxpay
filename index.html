Everything between the SMS and your server

Six features that turn a payment alert on your phone into a verified order on your backend.

SMS and notification engine

Captures MFS SMS and app notifications in real time, tracks the SIM slot, and lets you manage custom regex profiles.

Signed webhook dispatcher

Sends a JSON POST from your template, with Bearer, API-key or custom headers and an X-Enox-Signature.

{platform} {amount} {tx_id} {sender} {balance} {fee} {sim_slot} {source} {sms} {time}

Reliable in the background

Foreground service, battery optimization checks, auto-resume after boot, and automatic WorkManager retries.

Analytics, logs and CSV

See volume breakdowns, filter by SUCCESS, PENDING or FAILED, search live, recover from trash, and export CSV in one tap.

Telegram bot alerts

Get capture alerts, delivery confirmations and scheduled watchdog heartbeats in Telegram.

Privacy and security first

Lock the app with biometrics or a PIN, keep encrypted local backups, and verify the V2-signed APK before installing.

Verify every webhook in a few lines

Each request carries an event name, a timestamp and a signature. Compute the HMAC of the timestamp and raw body with your webhook secret, then compare.

X-Enox-EventEvent type, such as payment.received
X-Enox-TimestampUnix time the request was signed
X-Enox-Signature: sha256=…hmac_sha256(secret, "$timestamp.$rawBody")
